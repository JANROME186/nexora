# Business Architecture

This volume defines Nexora from the business-process perspective before translating requirements into modules, APIs, database entities or code.

Business Architecture is the source of truth for:

- Value streams.
- Business capabilities.
- Process maps.
- Patient, doctor and laboratory journeys.
- BPMN process definitions.
- Event Storming models.
- Business rules.
- Traceability from business intent to implementation artifacts.

Nexora must be built as a process-driven Healthcare Operations Platform, not as a collection of independent CRUD screens.
