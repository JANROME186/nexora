# Business Capabilities

Business capabilities describe what Nexora must be able to do independently of implementation technology, UI channel or deployment model.

Each capability must include:

- Business objective.
- Users/personas involved.
- Processes and subprocesses.
- Business rules.
- Domain relationship.
- Stories and use cases.
- APIs and events.
- Data entities.
- UX/mobile references.
- Tests and quality controls.
