# MOB-001 Patient Registration Mobile Screen

The mobile screen must work on low, medium and high-end Android/iOS devices using progressive capabilities.

## Core experience

- Lightweight form.
- Minimal network payload.
- Accessible inputs.
- Offline draft support when possible.

## Enhanced experience

- Camera-assisted document capture.
- Catalog autocomplete.

## Intelligent experience

- OCR-assisted identity capture when supported by the device and plan.
