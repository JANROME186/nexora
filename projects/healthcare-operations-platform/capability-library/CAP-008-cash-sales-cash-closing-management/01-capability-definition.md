# CAP-008 Capability Definition

## Purpose

Cash, Sales & Cash Closing Management enables Nexora to control the financial flow of diagnostic services from quotation and order payment to cancellation, refund, daily closing and audit.

## Business Goals

- Register sales linked to diagnostic orders, patients, branches and users.
- Support multiple payment methods and partial payments.
- Enforce permission-based discounts, cancellations and refunds.
- Provide accountable cash drawer opening, movement and closing.
- Reconcile expected amounts against collected amounts.
- Maintain immutable audit trails for financial operations.
- Provide the foundation for invoicing, accounting and executive analytics.

## Scope

Included:

- Sales for diagnostic orders.
- Payment capture.
- Discounts and promotions application.
- Cancellations and refunds.
- Cash drawer sessions.
- Cash movements.
- Cash closing by cashier and branch.
- Payment reconciliation.
- Financial audit trail.

Excluded from this capability:

- Fiscal invoice issuance; owned by Billing & Tax Compliance.
- Inventory cost accounting; owned by Inventory & Procurement.
- General ledger; future Accounting capability.
