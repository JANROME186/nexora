# Business Rules

| Rule ID | Rule | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| INV-BR-001 | Every stock item must belong to an organization and may optionally be scoped to a branch or warehouse. | High |
| INV-BR-002 | A branch cannot consume stock that is not available in its assigned warehouse unless inter-branch transfer is approved. | High |
| INV-BR-003 | Supplies with expiration date must be managed by lot. | High |
| INV-BR-004 | Expired lots cannot be consumed for clinical processing. | Critical |
| INV-BR-005 | Reagents linked to tests must be traceable to the result workflow when required by quality policy. | Critical |
| INV-BR-006 | Stock adjustments require a reason and permission. | High |
| INV-BR-007 | Purchase orders require approval when exceeding configured amount thresholds. | High |
| INV-BR-008 | Goods receipt cannot exceed purchase order tolerance unless authorized. | Medium |
| INV-BR-009 | Minimum stock alerts must be generated by branch, warehouse and item. | High |
| INV-BR-010 | Supplier status must be active before issuing purchase orders. | High |
| INV-BR-011 | Inventory movements must be immutable after posting; corrections require reversal movements. | Critical |
| INV-BR-012 | Stock costs must preserve historical values for audit and profitability analysis. | High |
| INV-BR-013 | Controlled supplies may require dual authorization before adjustment or disposal. | High |
| INV-BR-014 | Inventory audit findings must create traceable variance records. | High |
| INV-BR-015 | AI recommendations must never automatically purchase or dispose inventory without human approval. | Critical |
