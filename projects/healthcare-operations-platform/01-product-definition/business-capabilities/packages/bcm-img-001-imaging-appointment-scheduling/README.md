# BCM-IMG-001 — Imaging Appointment Scheduling Capability Package

**Spanish:** Agenda de Estudios de Imagen
**Domain:** DOM-06 Imaging
**Priority:** High
**Roadmap:** REL-004
**Bounded Context:** `imaging-operations`
**Primary Aggregate:** `ImagingAppointmentSlot` (`AGG-031`)

## Summary
Governs scheduling of diagnostic imaging procedures across modalities (CT, MRI, X-Ray, Ultrasound, Mammography), validating room availability, modality concurrency ceilings, contrast preparation lead times, and patient prerequisites.
