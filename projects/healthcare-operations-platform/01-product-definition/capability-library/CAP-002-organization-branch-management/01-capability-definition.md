# 01 Capability Definition

## Purpose

Organization & Branch Management enables Nexora to represent a diagnostic organization as a multi-tenant, multi-company and multi-branch structure.

The capability supports:

- Laboratory tenant registration.
- Legal organization profiles.
- Branch and diagnostic center administration.
- Hierarchical organizational structure.
- Operational areas and service units.
- Branch schedules and service availability.
- Multi-branch configuration.
- Branch-level permissions and operational isolation.
- Country and regulatory context assignment.

## Business Outcomes

1. A laboratory can operate one or many branches.
2. Each branch can have its own services, schedules, users, inventory and caja.
3. The organization can grow from one branch to a network without redesigning the platform.
4. Each tenant remains logically isolated from other tenants.
5. Country-specific behavior can be attached without modifying the core platform.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Tenant/laboratory profile.
- Legal entity profile.
- Branch profile.
- Branch operational status.
- Branch hierarchy and parent-child relationships.
- Branch services and capabilities.
- Branch contact data.
- Branch addresses.
- Branch schedules.
- Branch-level configuration.
- Organizational units.
- Position hierarchy baseline.

### Out of Scope

- User authentication implementation.
- Payroll.
- Accounting ledger.
- Detailed HR management.
- Medical service execution.
