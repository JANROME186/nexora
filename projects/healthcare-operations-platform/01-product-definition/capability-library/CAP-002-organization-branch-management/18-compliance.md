# 18 Compliance

## Compliance Considerations

- Branch legal and tax data must support country-specific rules.
- Branch address data may be considered personal/business-sensitive depending on jurisdiction.
- Audit records for organization changes must be retained according to policy.
- Branch service availability may affect clinical operations and must be traceable.
- Country Packs govern tax identifiers, address formats, currency and regulatory labels.

## Audit Requirements

Audit must capture:

- User.
- Tenant.
- Branch.
- Action.
- Previous value when applicable.
- New value when applicable.
- Timestamp.
- Correlation ID.
- Reason when required.
