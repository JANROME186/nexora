# AI Use Cases

## MVP-Compatible AI

- Summarize radiology report for patient-friendly explanation after release.
- Suggest report template based on imaging service and modality.
- Detect missing report sections before signature.
- Prioritize radiologist worklist using SLA, stat priority and pending time.
- Generate operational insights about modality utilization and no-shows.

## Future AI

- Computer vision pre-screening assistance.
- AI-assisted comparison with prior studies.
- Automated image quality assessment.
- Structured finding extraction.
- Voice dictation and transcription for radiology reports.

## Guardrails

- AI must never sign or release imaging reports.
- AI findings must be labeled as assistant-generated until reviewed.
- AI provider calls must be logged, cost-controlled and privacy-reviewed.
- Patient-facing explanations must avoid diagnosis beyond released report content.
