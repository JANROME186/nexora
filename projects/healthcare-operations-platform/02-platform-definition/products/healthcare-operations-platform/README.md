# Healthcare Operations Platform

## Product Owner

Nexora.

## Product Type

Healthcare SaaS and deployable enterprise platform.

## Initial Market

- Clinical laboratories.
- Imaging centers.
- Diagnostic healthcare organizations.
- Multi-branch laboratory networks.

## Access Areas

1. Public website.
2. Employee portal.
3. Doctor portal.
4. Patient portal.
5. Mobile applications where operationally justified.

## Scope

The platform covers:

- Patient management.
- Doctor management.
- Laboratory and branch management.
- Orders.
- Sample collection.
- Results.
- Imaging.
- Cash.
- Billing.
- Inventory.
- Quality.
- Integrations.
- Data migration.
- AI-assisted operations.
