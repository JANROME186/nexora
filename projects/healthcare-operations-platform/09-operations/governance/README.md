## Principles & Compliance

1. **Agent-Agnostic**: All governance rules, checklists, and escalation matrices are completely agent-agnostic and machine-parseable.
2. **Open-Source-First**: Leverages open observability, standard HTTP health probes, Actuator metrics, and standard ITIL/DevOps practices.
3. **Zero Lock-In**: Designed to operate without proprietary SaaS support tools or vendor lock-in.
