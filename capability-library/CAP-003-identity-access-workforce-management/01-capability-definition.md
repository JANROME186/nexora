# 01 Capability Definition

## Purpose

Identity, Access & Workforce Management enables Nexora to control who can access the platform, what they can do, where they can do it and under which organizational context.

The capability covers:

- Employee registration.
- User account provisioning.
- Role and permission management.
- RBAC + ABAC authorization.
- Branch-scoped access.
- Position and hierarchy-aware permissions.
- Authentication policies.
- Session and token management.
- Access review.
- User lifecycle: invite, activate, suspend, deactivate.
- Audit trail for access and administrative actions.

## Business Outcomes

- Every laboratory can create and manage its own employees and users.
- Access is always scoped by tenant, branch, role, permissions and policy attributes.
- Sensitive clinical, financial and administrative actions are auditable.
- Users see only the features and data they are allowed to access.
- The platform can support small labs, multi-branch organizations and enterprise chains.

## Scope MVP 1

Included:

- Employee profile.
- User invitation.
- Login contract.
- Roles.
- Permissions.
- Branch access assignments.
- Position-aware access.
- Basic MFA-ready architecture.
- Audit records.

Deferred:

- SSO federation.
- Passwordless login.
- Full HR management.
- Payroll.
- Advanced identity governance.
