# CAP-007 Capability Definition

## Purpose
Manage the complete lifecycle of laboratory and diagnostic results from the moment samples are ready for processing until reports are validated, signed, delivered and audited.

## Business Outcomes
- Reduce result turnaround time.
- Improve traceability of clinical validation.
- Prevent unauthorized result publication.
- Detect and escalate critical values.
- Generate consistent reports across branches and laboratories.
- Support result corrections with full version history.

## Scope
Included:
- Manual result entry.
- Result import from analyzers and integrations.
- Calculated results.
- Reference range evaluation.
- Critical value detection.
- Technical validation.
- Clinical validation.
- Digital signature workflow.
- PDF/structured report generation.
- Patient and doctor delivery.
- Result amendments and versioning.

Out of scope for this capability:
- Device interface implementation details, covered by Integration Architecture.
- Imaging interpretation, covered by Imaging Operations.
- Invoice/payment lifecycle, covered by Cashier/Billing capabilities.
