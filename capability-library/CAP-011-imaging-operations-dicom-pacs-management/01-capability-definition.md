# Capability Definition

## ID

CAP-011

## Name

Imaging Operations & DICOM/PACS Management

## Business Objective

Manage the complete imaging operation for diagnostic centers, including imaging appointments, rooms, modalities, technicians, acquisition workflow, DICOM study lifecycle, radiology interpretation, report signing, PACS storage, web viewing and secure delivery to patients and physicians.

## Business Value

This capability allows Nexora to operate beyond clinical laboratory workflows and support imaging services such as X-ray, ultrasound, mammography, CT, MRI and other diagnostic imaging modalities. It enables traceability, faster interpretation, standardized reporting, DICOM interoperability and secure access to images and reports.

## Users

- Imaging receptionist.
- Radiology technician.
- Radiologist.
- Referring physician.
- Patient.
- Imaging supervisor.
- Branch administrator.
- PACS administrator.
- Quality auditor.
- Support engineer.

## Scope

Included:

- Imaging service catalog linkage.
- Imaging appointments and room scheduling.
- Modality and room management.
- Technician worklist.
- DICOM study registration.
- PACS object metadata management.
- Radiology interpretation workflow.
- Report creation, validation, signature and delivery.
- Patient/doctor image access through viewer links.
- Audit trail for imaging access and report changes.

Excluded from MVP 1 unless enabled by license/feature flag:

- Full diagnostic web viewer with advanced measurement tools.
- AI-assisted imaging diagnosis.
- DICOM routing rules engine.
- Vendor-specific modality integrations.
- Long-term archive tiering automation.
