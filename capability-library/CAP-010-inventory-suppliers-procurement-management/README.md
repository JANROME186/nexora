# CAP-010 Inventory, Suppliers & Procurement Management

Capability package for managing inventory, suppliers, procurement, lots, expirations, branch stock, consumption, adjustments and operational cost control for Nexora.

This capability supports laboratory operations by ensuring that reagents, consumables, containers, imaging materials and operational supplies are available, traceable and controlled by organization and branch.

## Scope

- Supplier management.
- Product and supply catalog.
- Warehouse and stock management.
- Purchase requests and purchase orders.
- Goods receipt.
- Lot and expiration tracking.
- Stock movements and adjustments.
- Consumption by order, test, sample and branch.
- Minimum stock and reorder alerts.
- Cost traceability.
- Inventory audits.

## Primary dependencies

- CAP-002 Organization & Branch Management.
- CAP-003 Identity, Access & Workforce Management.
- CAP-005 Catalog & Test Configuration Management.
- CAP-006 Orders, Appointments & Sample Collection.
- CAP-007 Laboratory Results & Report Management.
- CAP-008 Cash, Sales & Cash Closing Management.
