# UI Specification

## Web Views

- Supplier list and detail.
- Inventory item catalog.
- Warehouse management.
- Stock balance dashboard.
- Lot expiration dashboard.
- Purchase request workflow.
- Purchase order workflow.
- Goods receipt form.
- Stock movement history.
- Inventory audit workspace.
- Reorder alerts board.

## UX Principles

- Simple tables with filters and export.
- Clear status badges for stock, lots and approvals.
- Low-resource compatible interface.
- Progressive enhancement for charts and AI recommendations.
- Role-based actions only.
