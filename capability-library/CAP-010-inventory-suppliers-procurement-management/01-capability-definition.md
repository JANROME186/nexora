# Capability Definition

## ID

CAP-010

## Name

Inventory, Suppliers & Procurement Management

## Business Objective

Control and optimize the availability, traceability, cost and consumption of all supplies required to operate laboratories, branches and diagnostic services.

## Business Value

This capability reduces stockouts, expired supplies, manual adjustments, procurement delays and operational cost leakage. It enables each branch to work with controlled inventory while headquarters maintains global visibility.

## Users

- Inventory manager.
- Branch administrator.
- Warehouse operator.
- Laboratory technician.
- Purchasing manager.
- Supplier manager.
- Finance supervisor.
- Organization administrator.
- Auditor.

## Channels

- Employee web portal.
- Administrative web portal.
- Mobile inventory assistant.
- Public/internal API.
- Integration connectors.
- AI assistant.

## Out of Scope for MVP 1

- Full accounting ledger integration.
- Automated supplier EDI.
- Predictive purchasing with external market prices.
- Robotic warehouse automation.
