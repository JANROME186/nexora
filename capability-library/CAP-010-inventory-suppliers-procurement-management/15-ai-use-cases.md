# AI Use Cases

## MVP Candidate AI Use Cases

- Suggest reorder quantities based on historical consumption.
- Detect unusual consumption patterns.
- Identify lots at risk of expiration.
- Explain stock variance trends to administrators.
- Suggest suppliers based on reliability and historical lead time.

## Guardrails

- AI must not execute purchases automatically.
- AI must not dispose or quarantine stock automatically.
- AI recommendations must include confidence, evidence and human approval.
- AI must respect tenant and branch boundaries.
