# Mobile Specification

## Mobile Use Cases

- Scan item or lot barcode.
- Receive goods.
- Count inventory during audits.
- Check stock availability.
- Report shortage.
- Record stock consumption.

## Device Strategy

The mobile inventory experience must support low and mid-range Android devices using simple flows, offline-tolerant forms and minimal heavy graphics.

## Offline Support

- Inventory count drafts may be stored locally.
- Barcode scan results may be queued.
- Sync conflicts must be resolved explicitly.
