# Patient Journey Event Storming

## Domain Events

| ID | Event | Description | Producer | Consumers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EVT-001 | PatientCreated | A patient record was created. | Patient Management | Audit, Notifications, Orders |
| EVT-002 | PatientIdentityConfirmed | Reception confirmed patient identity. | Patient Management | Order Management |
| EVT-003 | GuardianRegistered | A guardian was registered for a minor/dependent patient. | Patient Management | Compliance, Audit |
| EVT-004 | PatientConsentCaptured | Required consent was captured. | Patient Management | Orders, Compliance |
| EVT-005 | DiagnosticOrderCreated | A diagnostic order was created. | Order Management | Cashier, Sample Collection, Imaging |
| EVT-006 | PaymentRegistered | Payment for an order was registered. | Cashier | Orders, Billing |
| EVT-007 | OrderLabelsGenerated | Labels were generated for samples or studies. | Order Management | Sample Collection |
| EVT-008 | SampleCollectionQueued | The order was queued for sample collection. | Sample Collection | Notifications, Dashboard |
| EVT-009 | SampleCollected | A sample was collected and identified. | Sample Collection | Laboratory Operations, Inventory |
| EVT-010 | ImagingStudyQueued | Imaging study was queued. | Imaging Operations | Patient Portal, Dashboard |
| EVT-011 | ResultGenerated | A result was generated by manual entry or interface. | Result Management | Validation, Audit |
| EVT-012 | ResultValidated | A clinical result was validated. | Result Management | Patient Portal, Doctor Portal, Notifications |
| EVT-013 | ResultPublished | A result was published for secure access. | Result Management | Patient Portal, Doctor Portal |
| EVT-014 | CriticalResultDetected | A configured critical result was detected. | Result Management | Alerts, Doctors, Compliance |
| EVT-015 | PatientNotified | Patient notification was sent. | Notifications | Audit |

## Hotspots

- Duplicate patient detection.
- Legal guardian validation.
- Consent versioning.
- Critical result escalation.
- Offline or low-connectivity mobile usage.
- Multi-tenant audit separation.
