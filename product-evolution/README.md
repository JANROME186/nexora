# Product Evolution & SaaS Business Architecture

This volume defines how Nexora evolves as a SaaS product without breaking existing customers, deployments, APIs, data, integrations, or user experiences.

It governs:

- Product lifecycle
- Licensing and plans
- Feature flags
- Marketplace and plugins
- Country packs
- Healthcare packs
- Deprecation and compatibility
- Product rollout and release governance
- Capability and provider registries

Nexora must evolve as a platform, not as a collection of hard-coded modules.
