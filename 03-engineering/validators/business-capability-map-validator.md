# Business Capability Map Validator

## Rules

- BCM-VAL-001: Every capability must have a unique ID.
- BCM-VAL-002: Every capability must belong to exactly one business domain.
- BCM-VAL-003: Every capability must define Spanish and English names.
- BCM-VAL-004: Every capability must define priority.
- BCM-VAL-005: Every capability must define roadmap phase.
- BCM-VAL-006: Capability IDs must follow `BCM-XXX-999`.
- BCM-VAL-007: No capability may duplicate another capability's business purpose.
