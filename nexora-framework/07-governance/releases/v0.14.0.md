# Release v0.14.0

## Objective

Introduce the Business Architecture layer so Nexora is designed from business processes and capabilities before APIs, databases or screens.

## Added

- Enterprise Value Chain.
- Business Capability Model.
- Patient Journey.
- Patient Intake Process.
- Patient Journey Event Storming.
- Patient Journey Business Rules.
- Patient Journey Traceability Matrix.
- Business Architecture Agent.
- Create Business Capability Playbook.

## Impact

This release establishes that every implementation artifact must trace back to a business capability, business process, rule and user story. Future backlog, OpenAPI contracts and database design must reference this layer.

## Next Release Candidate

v0.15.0 should expand the business architecture into the first operational value stream: Patient Attention, including reception, cashier, order creation and sample routing.
