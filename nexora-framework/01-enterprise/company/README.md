# Nexora Company

## Definition

Nexora is a software development and Artificial Intelligence company.

Its purpose is to build AI-native, cloud-agnostic, enterprise-grade platforms that automate complex business operations.

## First Product

Nexora's first strategic product is the **Healthcare Operations Platform**, focused on diagnostic healthcare organizations such as laboratories and imaging centers.

## Company Areas

- Product Engineering.
- Artificial Intelligence.
- SaaS Platforms.
- Automation.
- Data Engineering.
- Cloud and On-Premise Solutions.
- Interoperability.
- Digital Transformation.
